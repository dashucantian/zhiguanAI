# 判语 009：反馈闭环定位为"镜子"非"训练器"

> 判决日期：2026-09-09 | 状态：已定 | 影响范围：产品叙事 / 宣发 / 反馈设计

## 判决

反馈闭环的价值主张定为"可观测、可复盘的大脑状态镜子"，不承诺"提升专注/深化禅修"的训练功效。校准与个体化升格为核心差异化主张。

## 依据

**主证（内部，2026-09-09 修订更换）**：法师 09-08 夜会——VR 是化城〔01:38:12〕；"止观定慧你修出来的时候，自带 VR，自带这个场景的反馈……这个 VR，它实际上是模拟的这个法界、这个场、常态"〔02:32:47〕；"对于少数人来说，你自己就可以做得到，就不需要依赖 VR、什么大模型"〔02:16:20〕。系统照见的是可观测状态，判定权在实修证量——会议决策 3：闭环有效性"以实质的修证、止观为标准，不以设备指标或论文切片为标准"。

**外部风险参照（降档定位，不作主证）**：
- JMIR 2025 元分析（Treves 等，MIT/哥大，预注册）：16 项 RCT／763 人＋5 项被试内设计，其中 11/16 用 Muse（即本项目现有设备）
- 心理痛苦仅小幅下降（k=11, Hedges g=−0.16, P=.03）；认知（g=0.07, P=.48）、正念（g=0.02, P=.83）、生理健康（g=0.11, P=.57）**均无对照优势**；脑靶标调制未获证据
- 该负向结果**反向支持决策 3**：消费级设备自报的"脑靶标调制"不可靠，故本就不该拿设备指标当验收标准
- 出处：https://www.jmir.org/2025/1/e68204/

## 被否方案

- 沿博客叙事宣称"反馈显著提升禅修质量 X%"——被元分析总体证据否定，且踩"承诺功效"的宣发与伦理陷阱
- 把闭环目标定为"训练可控性"——脑靶标调制本身在消费级证据里未成立

## 可复用教训

单篇阳性结论遇元分析阴性时，以元分析为准。负向裁决未必是坏消息：它替我们挡掉了功效陷阱，同时点名指出的短板（校准不足、缺个体化）恰是我们的机会点。此判决与法师一贯警惕"指标外求"、以及合作方"大脑状态镜子，不替代觉察内核"的自我表述同频。

## English Summary

**Decision:** Position the feedback loop as an observable, reviewable "mirror" of brain state, not a "trainer" that promises to improve focus or deepen meditation. Calibration and personalization become the core differentiation.

**Evidence:**
- JMIR 2025 meta-analysis (Treves et al., MIT/Columbia, pre-registered): 16 RCTs / 763 participants + 5 within-participant designs; 11/16 used Muse (our current device)
- Only a small drop in psychological distress (k=11, Hedges g=−0.16, P=.03); no control advantage for cognition (g=0.07, P=.48), mindfulness (g=0.02, P=.83), or physiological health (g=0.11, P=.57)
- No evidence for brain-target modulation — the core neurofeedback premise "practice → learn to control Alpha/Theta" did not hold
- Authors attribute effects possibly to "neurosuggestion" (neuro-tech placebo) and explicitly recommend more thorough calibration and device personalization

**Rejected Alternative:**
Claiming "feedback significantly improves meditation quality by X%" (refuted by overall meta-analytic evidence; falls into the efficacy-promise marketing/ethics trap); or defining the loop's goal as training controllability (not supported by consumer-grade evidence).

**Reusable Lesson:**
When a single positive study conflicts with a negative meta-analysis, trust the meta-analysis. A negative ruling isn't necessarily bad news — it shields us from the efficacy trap while the shortcomings it names (poor calibration, lack of personalization) are exactly our opportunity. Consistent with the abbot's caution against "outsourcing insight to metrics."
