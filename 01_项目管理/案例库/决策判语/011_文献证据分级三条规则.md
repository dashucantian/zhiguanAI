# 判语 011：文献证据分级三条规则

> 判决日期：2026-09-09 | 状态：已定 | 影响范围：数据治理 / 调研规范 / 案例库写作纪律

## 判决

为"从他人经验借鉴"确立三条证据分级规则，并入数据治理的文献分级规范：① 元分析优先于单篇；② 无仓库链接的"开源"声称降档为不可复现；③ 我方验证至少设伪反馈对照＋不良反应记录。

## 依据（可核验的数字）

- 规则①实证：本轮"AI 引导反馈显著提升状态正念"单篇（2026-02）与 JMIR 2025 元分析（含 16 RCT）总体阴性直接冲突，取元分析为准
- 规则②实证：文章一宣称 neuro-beauty-pipeline 开源但至今给不出仓库地址，相应数字（吻合度 89% 等）永久停留"量级参考"档
- 规则③实证：JMIR 指出多数研究拿冥想 App 当对照（缺假反馈组），21 项研究中 19 项未评估不良反应——设伪反馈对照是低成本超过文献平均水准的抓手
- 出处：04_研究调研\EEG与AI\20260907_借鉴项定向核验对照表_v1.md §四

## 被否方案

- 见阳性单篇即采信——本轮已两次被元分析/原始研究翻转（功效叙事、眨眼阈值）
- 见"开源"字样即认定可复现——无链接等于无仓库
- 我方将来只做"有反馈 vs 无反馈"两态——无法排除安慰剂，重蹈文献覆辙

## 可复用教训

借他人经验时，证据的"种类"比"结论方向"更重要：元分析压单篇、原始研究压博客、可复现压口头开源。把这三条写成制度，可避免每次重新判断。

## English Summary

**Decision:** Establish three evidence-grading rules for borrowing from others' experience, to be merged into the data-governance literature-tiering spec: (1) meta-analysis over single studies; (2) "open-source" claims without a repo link are downgraded to non-reproducible; (3) our own validation must at minimum include a sham-feedback control + adverse-effect logging.

**Evidence:**
- Rule 1: this round's single "AI-guided feedback improves state mindfulness" paper (2026-02) conflicted with the JMIR 2025 meta-analysis (16 RCTs); we took the meta-analysis
- Rule 2: Article 1 claimed an open neuro-beauty-pipeline but gave no repo link; its numbers (89% match etc.) stay permanently in "order-of-magnitude reference" tier
- Rule 3: JMIR noted most studies used meditation apps as controls (no sham group) and 19/21 didn't assess adverse effects — sham control is a cheap way to exceed the literature's average bar

**Rejected Alternative:**
Trusting any single positive study (flipped twice this round); treating the word "open-source" as reproducible (no link = no repo); comparing only "feedback vs no-feedback" (can't rule out placebo).

**Reusable Lesson:**
When borrowing experience, the *kind* of evidence matters more than the *direction* of the conclusion: meta-analysis beats single study, primary study beats blog, reproducible beats verbal "open-source." Codifying these three rules avoids re-judging each time.
