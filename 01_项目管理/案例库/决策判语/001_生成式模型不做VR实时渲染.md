# 判语 001：生成式模型不做 VR 实时渲染

> 判决日期：2026-09-05 | 状态：已定 | 影响范围：整体架构

## 判决

不能。生成式模型只做离线资产生产，实时反馈由 Shader 参数化驱动。

## 依据（可核验的数字）

- VR 一帧预算 16.7ms（90Hz），脑电驱动延迟须 <20ms
- 视频生成模型：秒至分钟级；3D 生成：分钟级
- 差距 3–5 个数量级，无优化空间

## 被否方案

用视频/世界模型实时生成画面（李飞飞 World Labs Marble 等）——
另因闭源、数据须离开本机，与本地隐私要求冲突。

## 可复用教训

先比数量级再谈优化。凡是"延迟差三个数量级"的方案，不是工程问题，是物理问题。

## English Summary

**Decision:** Generative models (video/world models) cannot be used for real-time VR rendering. They are only suitable for offline asset production. Real-time feedback must be driven by parameterized shaders.

**Evidence:**
- VR frame budget: 16.7ms (90Hz), EEG-driven latency must be <20ms
- Video generation models: seconds to minutes; 3D generation: minutes
- Gap: 3-5 orders of magnitude, no room for optimization

**Rejected Alternative:**
Using video/world models (e.g., Fei-Fei Li's World Labs Marble) for real-time scene generation. Additionally, these models are closed-source and require data to leave the local machine, conflicting with privacy requirements.

**Reusable Lesson:**
Always compare orders of magnitude before discussing optimization. If the latency gap is three orders of magnitude, it's a physics problem, not an engineering problem.
