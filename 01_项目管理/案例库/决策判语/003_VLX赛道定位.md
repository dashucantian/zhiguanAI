# 判语 003：VLX 赛道定位

> 判决日期：2026-09-06 | 状态：已定 | 影响范围：多模态模型选型

## 判决

VLX 系列是"物理 AI/具身智能"赛道，不做 VR 视觉生成。VLX-Seek 1.5（3B）可作为阶段 C 的低成本视觉上下文增强。

## 依据（可核验的事实）

- VLX 系列含三款：VLX-Flow（流式视频理解）、VLX-Seek（细粒度视觉感知）、VLX-Go（具身导航决策）
- VLX-VR 在 MINERVA 基准以 78.8% 登顶，但这是视频问答能力，不是视觉生成
- VLX-Seek 1.5（3B）2026 年 8 月已开源，可在本机运行
- 赛道定位：机器人看懂世界，不是生成世界

## 被否方案

- 用 VLX 做 VR 视觉生成：赛道不对，模型能力不匹配
- 全量引入 VLX 系列：过度设计，只需 VLX-Seek 3B

## 可复用教训

模型选型先看赛道定位，再看能力指标。"看懂世界"和"生成世界"是两条完全不同的技术路线。

## English Summary

**Decision:** The VLX series belongs to the "physical AI / embodied intelligence" track—it does not do VR visual generation. VLX-Seek 1.5 (3B) may serve as a low-cost visual context enhancement in Phase C.

**Evidence (verifiable facts):**
- VLX series includes three models: VLX-Flow (streaming video understanding), VLX-Seek (fine-grained visual perception), VLX-Go (embodied navigation)
- VLX-VR topped the MINERVA benchmark at 78.8%, but that is video *question-answering*, not visual generation
- VLX-Seek 1.5 (3B) was open-sourced in August 2026 and can run locally
- Track positioning: helping robots *see* the world, not *generate* it

**Rejected Alternatives:**
- Using VLX for VR scene generation: wrong track, capability mismatch
- Adopting the whole VLX series: over-engineering; only VLX-Seek 3B is potentially useful

**Reusable Lesson:**
When selecting models, check the track positioning first, then capability benchmarks. "Understanding the world" and "generating the world" are two completely different technical routes.
